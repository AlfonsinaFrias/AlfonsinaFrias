AI Kills Learning by Being ‘Good Enough’

Co-authored with Ashish Bhatia for AACSB Insights, this article explores how AI’s ability to produce “good enough” work can unintentionally hinder the learning process. We argue that meaningful learning requires friction. Drawing on our experiences with experiential learning, we explore how educators can design learning experiences that keep curiosity, critical thinking, and human reflection at the center.
